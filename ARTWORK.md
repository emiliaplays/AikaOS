# Artwork and branding

The selected AikaOS desktop wallpaper, lock-screen illustration, mascot logo and GRUB background were created with integrated image generation using user-supplied Aika references. Selected system icons were authored as SVG assets. The color ASCII portraits and Fastfetch configurations were supplied by the maintainer and are included unchanged as the release default/variants.

Generation prompts are preserved in `assets/branding/prompts.json`, `assets/branding/desktop-prompt.txt` and `assets/grub/prompt.txt`.

Artwork, character design, mascot branding, logos, ASCII art and screenshots are excluded from the repository's software license unless a separate asset license explicitly states otherwise. This repository does not grant a separate license to reuse third-party character designs or trademarks. Existing rights remain with their respective rights holders.

The wallpapers are 1672 × 941 pixels. The transparent mascot image is 1319 × 1193 pixels. The GRUB background is prepared at 1920 × 1080. No native-4K claim is made for the wallpapers.
